Magic Daily Art – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Magic Daily Art ("the app") shows illustrations from Magic: The Gathering. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the app does not collect, store or sell any personal data, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not ask for your name, e-mail, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored only on your device

Your settings (filters, widget options) and the card currently shown in the widget are saved on your device so the app can remember them. They are never sent to us. If you use Android's backup feature, Android may include them in your device backup, under your own Google account.

Card data and images (Scryfall)

Card information and images are loaded from Scryfall's public service. To do that, your device connects to Scryfall's servers, which, like any website, receive technical information such as your IP address, and the searches you type in Explore. This is handled under Scryfall's privacy policy. The app sends no personal data to Scryfall.

Donations (Ko-fi)

If you choose to donate, the app opens our page on Ko-fi in your browser. Payments are processed entirely by Ko-fi and its payment providers, under Ko-fi's privacy policy. The app never sees your payment information. Donating is optional and unlocks nothing in the app.

Support e-mails

If you write to us from the Support page, your e-mail app sends the message. We only use your e-mail address and message to answer you, and we don't share them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
